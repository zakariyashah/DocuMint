Project Overview: Green Campus Initiative

Project Name: Green Campus Initiative.
Project Lead: Aisha Khan.
Project Budget: ₹2,50,000.
Project Deadline: 15 December 2026.
Main Objective: Reduce campus electricity consumption by 20%.

Detailed Scope of Work:
The Green Campus Initiative is a comprehensive sustainability project designed to transition our university campus toward energy-efficient infrastructure and environmentally conscious operations. Spearheaded by Project Lead Aisha Khan, the core team will audit building power usage, install smart sub-metering systems, replace legacy lighting with high-efficiency LED arrays, and deploy rooftop solar photovoltaic pilots across administrative blocks.

Financial Plan & Milestones:
The approved total budget for this phase is ₹2,50,000, allocated across energy monitoring hardware, vendor installations, student awareness workshops, and independent compliance verification. All milestone deliverables must be successfully commissioned and reviewed ahead of the firm project deadline of 15 December 2026.

Target Outcomes:
The primary metric for project success is achieving a verified reduction in overall campus electricity consumption by at least 20% compared to the baseline fiscal year. Secondary goals include fostering student engagement in green technology, reducing utility expenses, and creating a blueprint for carbon-neutral academic institutions.
